A second home on Vasna Road

Little Millennium Saiyed Vasna is a neighbourhood preschool for children aged 2 to 6. We follow the Little Millennium Seven Petal curriculum, and we run it the way a family would: small groups, familiar faces and plenty of time to play.

A note from us
“We wanted a preschool close to home where parents could walk in, meet the teachers, and know their child is in good hands.”

Our centre sits behind the Panchmukhi Hanuman Temple in Saiyed Vasna, serving families across Vasna Road and Bhayli. As part of Little Millennium, we bring a tested national curriculum and teacher training to a small, local school.

Every day is planned around how young children actually learn: by playing, asking, making and doing things together.
