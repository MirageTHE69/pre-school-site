About us

Safe, fun & made for little explorers

We're a neighbourhood preschool on Vasna Road with the backing of Little Millennium, one of India's best-known preschool networks. Our days are built around play and group work, because that's how young children learn best. We notice what makes each child different, and we help them grow happy, social and confident.
